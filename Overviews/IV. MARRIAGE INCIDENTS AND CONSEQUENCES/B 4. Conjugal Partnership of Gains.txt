### CONJUGAL PARTNERSHIP OF GAINS  
	Nature and Concept: 
    o	A special type of partnership 
    o	No merger of separate properties; complete separation of capitals 
    o	Upon dissolution, the net gains or benefits shall be divided equally between them unless otherwise agreed marriage settlement. 
    o	Rules on partnership will apply (suppletory application)
	When applicable
    o	Applicable only to valid and voidable marriages and void marriages under Art 40, FC. 
    o	Civil Code - default regime 
    o	Family Code - only if agreed upon in a marriage settlement
	Properties INCLUDED
    o	Properties acquired through onerous title DURING the marriage using conjugal funds 
	Manner of acquisition - onerous 
	Source of funds - conjugal funds 
	Does not apply to a property purchased on installments prior to marriage and the payment was completed during marriage and paid partly by the conjugal funds (consider when was the full ownership was vested)
    o	Properties obtained through labor, industry, work or profession DURING marriage 
	Daily wages, periodic salaries, honoraria, professional fees, in come from business 
	Debts contracted during marriage in the exercise of industry or profession - obligation of the conjugal partnership
    o	Properties acquired through occupation DURING marriage (mode of acquisition of ownership) 
	Properties not owned by anybody (hunting, fishing) 
	Share in the hidden treasure
