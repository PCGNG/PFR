### SYLLABUS
Suit Among Members of the Same Family/ Necessity of Earnest Efforts toward Compromise
Art. 151 in rel. to Art. 2035, Civil Code of the Philippines

•	Hiyas Savings v. Acuna, G.R. NO. 154132. August 31, 2006, 500 S 514
•	Martinez v. Martinez, 461 S 562
•	Hontiveros v. RTC of Iloilo City, 309 S 340
•	Vda. De Manalo v. CA, 16 January 2001

---
### CODAL PROVISIONS
Article 151. No suit between members of the same family shall prosper unless it should appear from the verified complaint or petition that earnest efforts toward a compromise have been made, but that the same have failed. If it is shown that no such efforts were in fact made, the same case must be dismissed.

This rules shall not apply to cases which may not be the subject of compromise under the Civil Code.
