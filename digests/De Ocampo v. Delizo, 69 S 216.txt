De Ocampo v. Delizo, 69 S 216
Facts:
The case was an action for partition filed by the children of Nicolas Delizo's first marriage (to Rosa Villasfer, who died in 1909) against his second wife, Dorotea de Ocampo, and their children.

Issue: The central issue was determining which properties belonged to the first conjugal partnership and which to the second, particularly concerning several hectares of land acquired through the Public Land Law (Homestead Act).
