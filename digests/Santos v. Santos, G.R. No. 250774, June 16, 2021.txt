Facts:
The dispute involved the heirs of the deceased Jose Santos. Jose was first married to Josefa, with whom he had eight children. After Josefa's death, Jose married Maria D. Santos. During his marriage to Maria, Jose acquired a 694-square-meter property, which he later donated to Maria through a "Kasulatan ng Pagkakaloob Pala". 

After Jose's death, his children and grandchildren from his first marriage sought to partition the property, arguing it was part of Jose's estate. Maria claimed sole ownership based on the donation deed. 

Issue:

Ruling: 
Void Donation: The Court declared the donation by Jose to Maria as null and void because Article 87 of the Family Code of the Philippines expressly prohibits donations between spouses during their marriage, with minor exceptions not applicable in this case.

Community Property: Since the donation was void, the property was considered part of the absolute community of property of Jose and Maria's marriage, as it was acquired during their union. Upon Jose's death, the community technically dissolved. Maria automatically owned one-half of the property as her share of the community assets.
