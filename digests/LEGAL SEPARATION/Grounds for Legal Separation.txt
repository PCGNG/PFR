*1. Infliction of Physical Violence*
  •	“Repeated physical violence or grossly abusive conduct directed against the petitioner, a common child, or a child of the petitioner” [Art. 55 (1)]
  •	If the physical violence is in the form of an attempt against the life of the petitioner, the physical violence need not be repeated for it to be a ground for legal separation

*2. Other grossly abusive conduct*
  •	Need not be physical. 
  •	Repeated acts of psychological and sexual violence and verbal abuse 
  •	Psychological violence – acts or omissions causing or likely to cause mental or emotional suffering of the victim

NOTE: (For 1 and 2) Repeated Physical Violence / Grossly Abusive Conduct 
   - directed against the petitioner, a common child, or a child of the petitioner 
   - must point to a pattern rather than an isolated minor event

*3.	Moral Pressure*

NOTE: Physical Violence or Moral Pressure to Change Religious or Political Affiliation 
   - forces a spouse to abandon core, deeply held beliefs under psychological duress 
   - the violence may only be a single act

*4.	Attempt to Corrupt or Induce into Prostitution*
   - applies to the petitioner, a common child, or a child of the petitioner, including connivance in such corruption

*5.	Final Judgment and imprisonment of more than Six Years Imprisonment*
    •	even if subsequently granted a pardon, the severity of the criminal conviction and sentence justifies separation
    •	Requisites: 
          o	Sentence imposed is imprisonment of more than 6 years
          o	The conviction occurs only after the celebration of the marriage

*6.	Drug Addiction, Habitual Alcoholism, Lesbianism or Homosexuality*
    •	To be ground for legal separation: must arise only after the celebration of marriage
    •	if concealed prior to marriage - annulment on the ground of fraud 
    •	if rooted in the history of the person even if it manifested after the celebration of marriage - nullity on the ground of psychological incapacity

*7.	Contracting a Subsequent Bigamous Marriage*
    •	whether committed within or outside the Philippines, violating the essential monogamous nature of civil marriage 
    •	without prejudice to criminal case

*8.	Sexual Infidelity or Sexual Perversion*
    •	Civil Code: Adultery and concubinage
    •	FC: Sexual infidelity – any sexual act short of the sexual intercourse
    •	existing or discovered during the marriage 
    •	if concealed prior to marriage - annulment on the ground of fraud 
    •	if rooted in the history of the person even if it manifested after the celebration of marriage - nullity on the ground of psychological incapacity

*9.	Attempt on the Life of Spouse*
    •	Requires the presence of intent to kill

*10.	Abandonment*
    •	Is the act of one spouse voluntarily separating from the other, with the intention of not returning to live together as husband and wife, that continues for the length of time required by statute.
    •	Requisites:
            1.	Without justifiable cause
            2.	For more than a year
  Note: 
    3 months – prima facie presumed to have no intention of returning
    More than 1 year – abandonment to be a ground for legal separation
