### Facts:
Elisea Laperal filed a petition to change her name from Elisea L. Santamaria to Elisea Laperal in view of the fact that she has been legally separated from Mr. Enrique R. Santamaria and has likewise ceased to live with him for many years, it is desirable that she be allowed to change her name and/or be permitted to resume using her maiden name.

The petition was opposed by the City Attorney of Baguio on the ground that the same violates the provisions of Article 370 (should be 372) of the Civil Code:
	
ART. 372. When legal separation has been granted, the wife shall continue using her name and surname employed before the legal separation. 

### Issue:
Whether a legally separated wife is permitted to revert to her maiden name. NO

### Ruling: 
The Court emphasized the mandatory nature of Article 372 of the Civil Code, which requires a legally separated wife to continue using her married name. This rule reflects the policy that legal separation does not sever the marital bond, and the wife's civil status remains "married". The Court held that this specific law takes precedence over the general provisions for a change of name under Rule 103. The Court found no sufficient justification for the change, stating that legal separation alone is not a valid reason to circumvent Article 372. Furthermore, the Court noted that the conjugal partnership was already dissolved and liquidated by the decree of legal separation in 1958, addressing any potential financial confusion.
